# Copywriter findings (CW)

**Brief change applied:** the owner has withdrawn the Punjabi-flavoured voice. Every Punjabi word or phrase is listed below (CW-01 to CW-26) with an exact English replacement. All the proposed copy in this file follows the new rule: fun, warm, a bit quirky, clear Indian English. "Railgaddi" and रेलगाड़ी stay.

## 1. Summary
- The brand name is spelled the same everywhere (49 × "Railgaddi", never "Railgaadi"), and the factual copy (stories, facts, sources, estimates) is careful and honest. But the landing never says in plain words what the site does: that line exists only inside `<noscript>`.
- 26 Punjabi or Punjabi-tinged items sit in headings, the H1, placeholders, toasts, the loading screen and the `<title>`. "Koi gal nahi" alone shows up 5 times, and twice in a row on the way from Home to Munnar.
- Several things are counted or named differently in different places: 822 vs 286 places, "8h 25m" over a train that takes 9h 15m, Discover vs Stories, Saved vs "Your trips", halts vs stops, "2 h" vs "1h 19m". Unknown links and 404 get no message that stays on screen.

### 2b. Voice guide (under the new rule)

1. **Plain Indian English, warm and a bit quirky. No Punjabi.** Do: "No station? No problem." Don't: "No station? Koi gal nahi."
2. **Pan-Indian words only when they're obvious from context and add warmth. At most one per screen, never in a label, button, placeholder or aria-label.** OK: "Pack the parathas" (the sentence explains itself). Don't: "Mausam" as the only label on a button; "Your station or city, ji".
3. **Say what it is before you joke.** Every first screen opens with one plain line about what you can do. Do: "Pick your station: the map lights up every place a direct train can take you." Then the fun.
4. **Jokes go on top of facts, never in place of them.** A joke never changes or hides a time, day, distance or station. Do: "Next train, today · 21:50 → 07:05 · 9h 15m". Don't: "Arrives Hampi" when you get off at Hosapete.
5. **One name per thing, everywhere.** Saved (the heart; its sections are Bucket list and Trips and routes), Stories (the button, Discover page and title), Weather, stops (not halts, except "5m halt"), Use where I am. Don't: "Your trips" in one place and "Saved" in another.
6. **Railway jargon gets a gloss the first time it appears.** Do: "Local trains (passenger, MEMU and DEMU)"; "live times on NTES". Don't: "Punjab · ASR" or "BSB · BCY · BSBS" on their own.
7. **One number style.** Durations always as `fmtMins` ("2h", "8h 25m", "74h 20m"), distances "402 km", heights "1,468 m", temperatures "34°", times 24-hour "21:50", chances "70% chance of rain". In prose, numerals for every measurement ("58 tunnels", "2 km").
8. **Arrows mean "go somewhere".** "See its stops →" opens another view. Expanding in place gets no arrow: "Show all 286". Back links start with "← ". External links end with " ↗".
9. **Errors say what happened and what to do next, in one breath, with no blame.** Do: "We couldn't load the timetable. Check your connection and try again." Don't: "Oops", "Oho", "sadly" stacked up.
10. **Sentence case; curly quotes in prose (‘ ’ “ ”); a colon to introduce, a full stop to end.** Do: "Dudhsagar means ‘sea of milk’." Don't: mix 'straight' and “curly” quotes.
