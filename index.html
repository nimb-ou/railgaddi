      About Railgaddi

      Pick where you start and see every place in India a train can take you. Pick a place without a station, like Munnar or Manali, and see the stations to take a train to and the road from each. Plan a trip with several stops, and see the weather where you're going.

      Timetable: Indian Railways' Trains at a Glance 2026 for most long-distance trains. Trains it doesn't cover (passenger, MEMU, DEMU and the hill railways' toy trains) keep the timetable published on data.gov.in (December 2017, Government Open Data License); each train says which. Other running days, and trains too new for either, come from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). Times change: check NTES before you travel, and tell us what's wrong.

      Places: guides from Wikivoyage (CC BY-SA 4.0), summaries from Wikipedia and landmarks from Wikidata (CC0). Photos from Wikimedia Commons, credited on each place. Towns from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0) and airports from OurAirports. Road distances from OpenStreetMap, routed with OSRM, for the places people look up most; elsewhere, and road times everywhere, are estimates.

      Weather: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0).

      Map: stations © OpenStreetMap contributors and datameet; India outline and state boundaries per Survey of India, via datameet/maps.

      Railgaddi is an independent project. It isn't affiliated with Indian Railways or IRCTC, and doesn't sell tickets. Its code is open source (MIT licence); the data keeps its sources' licences.

      Privacy: no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. What you save stays on your device. If you sign in with Google to keep it on every device, we store Google's account number for you, your first name and picture, and what you saved, never your email; delete it all any time from Saved. "Use my location" uses your position only on your device. The weather is fetched from Open-Meteo for the places you look at (their coordinates, never yours); a place name you search for that isn't in our list is looked up with Open-Meteo's place search; summaries of places without a station come from Wikipedia. Photos load from Wikimedia Commons.
