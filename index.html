    About the data

    Timetable: the Indian Railways timetable published on data.gov.in, December 2017. This is a prototype: trains introduced since then (every Vande Bharat, for one) are missing and running days aren't known. Check NTES before you travel.

    Stations: © OpenStreetMap contributors, and datameet.

    Places: guides from Wikivoyage (CC BY-SA 4.0) and landmarks from Wikidata (CC0). Photos from Wikimedia Commons, credited on each place.

    Map: India outline per Survey of India, via datameet/maps.
