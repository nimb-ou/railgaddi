          About Railgaddi

          Pick where you start and see every place in India a train can take you without changing. Or pick where you want to go and see every place with a direct train there. Each comes with photos and what's worth seeing.

          Timetable: Indian Railways' Trains at a Glance 2026 for most long-distance trains. Trains it doesn't cover, mostly passenger and local ones, keep the timetable published on data.gov.in (December 2017, Government Open Data License); each train says which. Other running days, and trains too new for either, come from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). Times change: check NTES before you travel, and tell us what's wrong.

          Places: guides from Wikivoyage (CC BY-SA 4.0) and landmarks from Wikidata (CC0). Photos from Wikimedia Commons, credited on each place.

          Map: stations © OpenStreetMap contributors and datameet; India outline per Survey of India, via datameet/maps.

          Railgaddi is an independent project. It isn't affiliated with Indian Railways or IRCTC, doesn't sell tickets, and doesn't track you.
